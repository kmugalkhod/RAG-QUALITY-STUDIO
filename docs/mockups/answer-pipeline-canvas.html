What changes

  • Fit runs after nodes load and are measured, not on the empty mount. It reruns when the panel opens or closes and on Auto-layout.
  • The graph is centered. The canvas fills the full height left under the toolbar.
  • Tools sit in a 48px left rail. Zoom and fit share one cluster with a readable zoom level. A minimap shows on desktop only.
  • Ctrl + scroll zooms. Plain scroll still pans or scrolls the page, as today.
  • Each node shows a ✓ or ! badge, so problems show on the card itself.

Why A

  • Keeps spec 0002's vertical 288×80 cards and the 320px panel, so no redesign is needed.
  • The five-node graph is linear, and a vertical flow suits the 320px panel.
  • Touches the fewest components: the canvas wrapper, the toolbar and the node badge.

Trade-offs

  • On wide screens a lot of horizontal space stays unused. That is acceptable for five nodes.
  • The minimap adds little for five nodes. It is optional and could be left out.

The graph runs left to right across the full canvas width, which suits wide desktops. Settings open as a floating drawer when you select a node, so the canvas keeps its full width. Validation and the saved version move to a bottom status bar.

Pros

Cons
